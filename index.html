Journal article 2026 Peer reviewed

TARG-YOLO: an efficient small target detection framework for UAV

Yan Du, Zifeng Dai, Teng Wu, Changzhen Hu, Shengjun Wei

Machine Vision and Applications · 37(2), Article 35 · DOI 10.1007/s00138-026-01792-2

A YOLOv9-based detector built for small UAVs in complex scenes. RepMS shifts attention toward informative regions, DDetHead reduces background redundancy, and TARG-IoU balances precise localization with sensitivity to tiny targets.
