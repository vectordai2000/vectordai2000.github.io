Zifeng Dai

Graduate student, Beijing Institute of Technology

School of Cyberspace Science and Technology

I study how swarms of UAVs coordinate and learn together — and how to do it without exposing what each node sees. My work sits between networked systems and machine learning: multi-agent reinforcement learning for hybrid action spaces, and privacy-preserving federated learning over non-IID data.

Two first-author papers came out of this, both with public code: MAPA-MARL in IEEE TVT 2026 and PQ-FRL in IEEE IoT-J 2026. The notes behind them — including what did not fit in the papers — are on this site.

Selected publications

Research page ↗

  1. IEEE TVT2026

    Meta-Policy-Based Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning for Dynamic UAV Networks: Resolving Hybrid Action Spaces and Non-Stationarity

    Zifeng Dai, Hui Xie, Shengjun Wei, Changzhen Hu

    A constrained MARL framework for UAV-assisted edge networks: a PID-Lagrangian for steady-state fairness, a dual-head actor that separates continuous flight from discrete offloading, and an asynchronous opponent model learned without runtime parameter sharing. Holds 79.1% QoS at 80 devices with an 18.5 MB on-device footprint.

    DOI ↗ Paper note → Code ↗

  2. IEEE IoT-J2026

    PQ-FRL: A Privacy-Preserving and Quality-Aware Federated Reinforcement Learning for UAV-Assisted Edge Computing

    Zifeng Dai, Hui Xie, Shengjun Wei, Changzhen Hu

    Client-side differential privacy with quality-aware aggregation: the server weights each update by a DP-protected local reward instead of a reputation heuristic, so weak reports from hard environments stop masquerading as noise. Holds 2.6 s average latency under ε = 1.0 and non-IID hotspots, for under 0.1% extra communication.

UAV networks & edge computing

Multi-agent reinforcement learning

Hybrid action spaces, non-stationary peers, and constrained optimization for agents that have to cooperate.

Federated learning

Aggregation that survives non-IID data, stale clients, and updates that look bad because they come from hard places.

Privacy & security

Differential privacy and poisoning resistance — and an honest account of what the guarantee costs in utility.
